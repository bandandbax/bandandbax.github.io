Our Ceremony

We're doing things a little differently!

Our ceremony will take place on Friday, July 23, with our main celebration following on Saturday, July 24.

Our ceremony venue is small and intimate, with space for approximately 30 guests. We'd love to have anyone who wants to join us, so ceremony spots will be available on a first-come, first-served basis.

We know that attending on Friday may mean taking time off work and staying an extra night, so there is absolutely no expectation that you attend both days. Come for the ceremony and celebration, or just join us for the big party on Saturday — whichever works best for you.

If you'd like to attend the ceremony, simply let us know when you RSVP. Once we've reached capacity, we'll close the ceremony RSVP.

Most importantly, we can't wait to celebrate with you on Saturday!
